Bank Marketing Analysis

An end-to-end data science project exploring the factors associated with the outcome of a bank marketing campaign.

Project Overview

This project explores a publicly available bank marketing dataset to investigate the relationship between direct marketing activity and customer suscription to a term deposit. The data relates to telephone-based direct marketing campagins conducted by a Portuguese banking institution. The dataset contains 45,211 observations, with customer characteristics and campaign information used to determine whetehr a client subscribed to a term deposit. The target variable, y, records the subscription outcome as yes or no. I selected this dataset because I work within the financial service sector and wanted to explore a data science problem that was relevant to my professional interests while using publicly available data.

Research Question

Which customer and campaign characteristics are associated with whether a customer subscribes to a term deposit following a bank marketing campaign?

Project Process

I used a structured process to take the project from raw data through to final findings. I first assessed the quality and structure of the dataset before preparing it for analysis. I then explored patterns within the data, developed an appropriate model and created visualisations to communicate the results and answer the research question.
