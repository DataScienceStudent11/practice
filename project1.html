DATA SCIENCE PROJECT

Bank Marketing Analysis

An end-to-end data science project exploring the customer and campaign characteristics associated with the outcome of a bank marketing campaign.

📊

Project Overview

This project explores a publicly available bank marketing dataset to investigate the relationship between customer characteristics, direct marketing activity and customer subscription to a term deposit.

The data relates to telephone-based direct marketing campaigns conducted by a Portuguese banking institution. The dataset contains 45,211 observations and 17 variables, including customer characteristics, campaign information and the subscription outcome.

The target variable, y, records whether the client subscribed to a term deposit as either yes or no.

I selected this dataset because I work within the financial services sector and wanted to explore a data science problem relevant to my professional interests while using publicly available and anonymised data.

Research Question

Which customer and campaign characteristics are associated with whether a customer subscribes to a term deposit following a bank marketing campaign?

Project Process

I used a structured process to take the project from raw data through to final findings. I first assessed the quality and structure of the dataset before preparing it for analysis. I then explored patterns within the data, developed an appropriate model and created visualisations to communicate the results and answer the research question.
