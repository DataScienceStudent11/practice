Bank Marketing Analysis

An end-to-end data science project exploring the factors associated with the outcome of a bank marketing campaign.

Project Overview

This project uses a publicly available bank marketing dataset to investigate which customer and campaign characteristics are associated with whether a customer subscribes following a marketing campaign.

The project follows an end-to-end data science process, beginning with raw public data and progressing through data quality assessment, data preparation, exploratory analysis, modelling, visualisation and interpretation of the findings.

Research Question

Which customer and campaign characteristics are associated with whether a customer subscribes following a bank marketing campaign?

Project Process

Raw Public Data → Clean & Prepare → Analyse & Model → Visualise → Explain Findings → Publish
